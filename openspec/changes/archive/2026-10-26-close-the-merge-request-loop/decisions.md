| D2 | May the loop commit and push fixes on the person's branch? | agent_resolved | Yes, one commit per round, message `fix(review): answer the PR #<n> review round <r>`, pushed before the re-review and the reply; test paths the guard's predicate names are reverted from the staged tree and spoken; a round that leaves nothing staged pushes nothing, posts every finding as open and ends the loop.
