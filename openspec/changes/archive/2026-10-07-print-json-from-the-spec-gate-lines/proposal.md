## Why

The `/interlock:spec` flow reaches three Interlock verdicts, and the skill lines that reach them print prose. `skills/spec/SKILL.md:197` runs `interlock ledger "<name>"`, `:210` runs `interlock validate "<name>"`, and `skills/review-artifacts/SKILL.md:122` runs `interlock gate --findings … --metrics <change>`. What crosses the Bash tool is `DECISIONS BLOCKING — 3 row(s): 1 needs_human, …` (`lib/ledger.mjs:433-435`), `NOT READY — <change>` with `problem:` lines (`lib/artifacts.mjs:305-316`), and `GATE BLOCKED — 1 blocker(s) of 2 finding(s)` with a `metrics:` line (`lib/findings.mjs:314-339`, `bin/interlock:1861-1863`). A reader of that output, the model included, has no field to read; it has a sentence to parse. The deep-research report (`reports/Interlock mods beyond the ship meter.md`; `research_notes/Interlock mods beyond the ship meter/spec_mod_feasibility.md` §2 and §6; `candidate_mod_catalog.md` candidate 5) found that this is the one thing standing between the spec flow and a machine-readable contract: every other line the flow runs already asks for JSON (`openspec status … --json` at `skills/spec/SKILL.md:123`, `interlock drift --json` at `:94`, `interlock validate [change-name] --json` at `skills/review-artifacts/SKILL.md:19`, `interlock ready … --json` at `skills/spec/continuity.md:16`).

The CLI already has the flag. `bin/interlock:1785` reads `const json = Boolean(flags.json)` once for every subcommand, and all three branches print through `emit(json, obj, text)` (`:961`): `ledger` at `:2240-2244`, `validate` at `:1872`, `gate` at `:1864`. `test/spine/cli.test.mjs` already parses each one's JSON (`:851`, `:1628`, `:449-458`). The research suspected `interlock validate` had no `--json`; it does. So the change is the skill lines, one sentence each on how to read the result, and the pins.

The pins are the other half. `test/skills.test.mjs` pins `--metrics` on the gate line (`:468-501`), the order of `interlock notify checkpoint` before `GOAL MET` (`:651-664`), and the continuity tokens (`:747-769`). It pins nothing about `openspec new change`, `openspec status … --json`, `interlock drift --json`, `interlock ledger`, `interlock validate` in the spec skill, or either `interlock autonomy` line (the only `openspec` matches in the file are `:316` and `:624`, unrelated). `docs/11-the-indicators.md:40-51` records what that costs: `--metrics` existed on `interlock review` for a year and no skill ever passed it, and an empty corpus reads exactly like a loop that never ran. `.claude/CLAUDE.md` states the rule: a prose instruction nobody asserts silently stops running. The sibling change `observe-the-spec-run-live` is a hooks module that will key on exactly these lines off the Bash tool results, so the day it lands without pins is the day a reword can blind it without failing anything.

One docs row is stale on the same path. `docs/10-agentic-workflow-ship-and-spec.md:191` lists `.claude/ready/<name>-review.json` as the continuity handoff; nothing under `lib/`, `bin/`, `skills/`, `hooks/` or `test/` writes or names that path, and the `--review` input it belonged to is deprecated (`bin/interlock:2261-2266`, `skills/spec/continuity.md:21`). `docs/05-continuity.md:18` still shows the deprecated flag.

## What Changes

- **Three skill lines ask for JSON.** `interlock ledger "<name>" --json` and `interlock validate "<name>" --json` in `skills/spec/SKILL.md` (§3a, §4); `interlock gate --findings … --metrics <change> --json` in `skills/review-artifacts/SKILL.md`. `--metrics <change>` stays on the gate line, in place, so the existing pin and the metrics record are untouched. No other line changes.
- **Each line says, in one sentence, which field is the verdict and which list to act on.** Ledger: `blocking` is the verdict, the `needs_human` entries of `rows` and the entries of `invalidRows` are what to report, and `missing` and `unparseable` are why a ledger with no rows still blocks. Validate: `ready` is the verdict, `problems` is what to fix, and an `error` with `candidates` means the name did not resolve. Gate: `passed` is the verdict, `blockers` and `malformed` are what to report, `metrics.written` with `metrics.reason` says whether the record landed, and `autonomyOutcome.blockers` is the `<n>` the spec skill passes to `interlock autonomy record`. The exit code remains the verdict on every line; the field is the same fact in a form a reader can take without parsing a sentence. No number is compared in prose.
- **The autonomy record stops counting from prose.** Today the spec skill's `interlock autonomy record review-artifacts --blockers <n>` takes `<n>` from the review's own `SUMMARY: N blockers` line, a count written by the party being gated (`skills/review-artifacts/SKILL.md:94`). `lib/findings.mjs:308-309` already labels `autonomyOutcome` as "what the caller feeds straight back into `interlock autonomy record`"; the spec skill now reads it from the gate's JSON. The "Keep the blocker and warning counts" sentence at `skills/spec/SKILL.md:233` is reworded to name what continuity and the autonomy record actually take: the findings file and `autonomyOutcome.blockers`.
- **The lines an observer keys on are pinned by token, in the flow's order.** `test/skills.test.mjs` gains anchored command-line pins for `openspec new change`, `openspec status --change … --json`, `interlock drift --json`, `interlock ledger … --json`, `interlock validate … --json` (in both skills), `interlock gate … --json`, both `interlock autonomy` lines and the `--json` already on the continuity `interlock ready` line; the field tokens above; the prohibition on re-deriving the verdict in prose; and the order drift → new change → status → ledger → validate → review-artifacts → autonomy → notify checkpoint → `GOAL MET`. Pins match the fenced command line, not a mention of it in prose, so the existing `interlock ledger` mention at `skills/spec/SKILL.md:191` cannot satisfy the ledger pin.
- **The CLI side is pinned, not changed.** `test/spine/cli.test.mjs` gains a test that `validate --json` on a change with no checkbox tasks exits 1 with `ready: false` and `problems` naming the cause, and that `gate --json` carries `autonomyOutcome.blockers` equal to `blockers.length`. Every exit code stays where it is: `ledger` exits 1 on `blocking` (`bin/interlock:2250`), `validate` on `!ready` (`:1873`), `gate` on `!passed` (`:1865`), and an unresolved change name exits 1 with `{ error, candidates }` on all three (`:1742-1753`).
- **Docs and the changelog.** `docs/10-agentic-workflow-ship-and-spec.md` steps 7-9 show the `--json` form and the field each reads; the `.claude/ready/<name>-review.json` row becomes the artifact review's findings file, `.claude/metrics/review-artifacts-<change>-<ts>.json`, consumed by `interlock gate` and, under `--findings`, by `interlock ready` for continuity. `docs/05-continuity.md:18` shows `--findings` instead of the deprecated `--review`. `CHANGELOG.md` Unreleased carries the change.
- **Named follow-ups, not done here.** A machine-readable spec trace on disk is a CLI job with an `interlock report` reader on day one (`interlock notify` is pure transport and writes nothing: `lib/notify.mjs:8-12`); the spec meter itself is `observe-the-spec-run-live`, which depends on this change; `openspec validate` and the two `interlock autonomy` lines keep their text output.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `spec/machine-readable-gate-lines`: the three Interlock gate lines the spec flow runs request JSON without moving their exit codes; each is accompanied by a sentence naming its verdict field and its list to act on; the lines an observer of the flow keys on, their field tokens and their order are pinned by token in the skill suite.

### Modified Capabilities

- `spec/continuity-provenance`: the continuity procedure's pinned-token requirement gains `--json` beside `interlock ready` and `--findings`, so the readiness verdict crosses the Bash tool as JSON on the one path where the spec flow hands over to ship. The line already carries the flag; the requirement and the pin catch its removal.

`spec/decision-ledger`, `review/evidence-gate`, `review/metrics-emission` and `report/non-gating` are read and untouched: the audit's semantics, the gate's severity and dismissal rules, the metrics record and its `--metrics <change>` pin, and the report's contract do not change. `--json` is a rendering of a verdict those capabilities already fix.
