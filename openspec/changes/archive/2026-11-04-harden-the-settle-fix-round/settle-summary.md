## Summary
- **Why:** a settle fix round committed and pushed the fixer's tree before anything verified it. On PR #30 that pushed a red head that CI caught, not the loop. The same pass posted a 28-row round-0 comment (26 suggestions) ahead of the two warnings that mattered, and ran a 49-minute unbounded `general-purpose` fixer.
- A fix round now runs the inter-wave verification (`verify plan/exec/judge --context inter-wave`) over its staged paths before it commits. A red round commits nothing, pushes nothing, prints `ROUND FAILED: unit red` and stops the loop early. A round the plan could not verify (no profile, or no unit command) now says so with `ROUND UNVERIFIED` (round 1 of this PR's review).
- `interlock settle comment` folds suggestions into one `<details>` block under the blockers and warnings. The fixer is a new bounded `interlock:settle-fixer` agent type with no `Agent` or `Skill` tool. A refused or unknown spawn now stops the round with `ROUND FAILED: fixer type unavailable` (round 1).
- The settle meter files a round's verify lines inside that round, `review-code`'s depth banner reports its dimension counts, and four prose claims that contradicted the code are corrected. Also carries the archive of `run-without-a-user-installed-openspec` (856a5a6).
