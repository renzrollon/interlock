## Why

The ship meter draws a run as a flat list. `drawPane` in `hooks/mod.mjs:409-479` prints one `wave-row-<label>` line per spawn it has seen (`:422-434`), keyed by the spawn's label and carrying its title, kind, routed model, served model, effort and state, with nothing that says which wave or batch the lane belongs to, what it waits on, or what is still to come. The sibling change `draw-wave-plan-and-handoff-graph-from-the-cli` builds the renderer that can say all of that: `lib/draw-plan.mjs`, pure and Node-free, drawing one bordered block per wave keyed by `waveIndex`, one row per lane with its batch, ids, model, tier, effort and `←dependsOn` ids, a verify-gate cell per wave boundary and a `then:` tail, cut at a given width so the pane's `wrap: 'truncate-end'` and the CLI's cut agree (its `proposal.md:11-14`). The deep-research note that recommended both changes worked the pane board out from the real halted run and found that it needs exactly four facts the pane does not have (`research_notes/Interlock mods beyond the ship meter/ship_wave_and_handoff_diagrams.md`, key question 6, Inferences): `waveIndex`, `batchIndex`/`batchCount`, the per-lane task ids, and the `dependsOn` edges.

The relay is the blocker, and it was named as one. On the Workflow host every step reaches the driver through a model that retypes the CLI's stdout, so `relayStep` (`lib/run.mjs:454-468`) hands that host only the whitelist `RELAY_STEP_FIELDS` (`:431-443`): `schema`, `action`, `then`, `spawns`, `banners`, `change`, `wave`, `reason`, `pingModel`, and each spawn less its inline `prompt`. The wave-state positions the step carries elsewhere, `waveIndex`, `batchIndex`, `batchCount` and `waveKind` (`PASS_THROUGH`, `:1104-1129`), and the batch's `lanes` (`:1244`) are stripped; `test/spine/run.test.mjs:3039-3091` pins the strip, and `test/fixtures/mod/steps.mjs:33-41` holds the relayed shape beside the full one so the kit exercises both. The meter therefore cannot place a spawn in a wave or a batch on the one host that runs a ship (`docs/07-cli-and-configuration.md:74` says so to the person), and no step on any host carries the plan's structure or the ids a batch completed. The archived meter design recorded the choice: "Widening the whitelist is a separate decision with its own cost (every relayed step grows) and is not taken here" (`openspec/changes/archive/2026-10-05-draw-the-ship-run-live/design.md:61`). This change takes it, the way that change made `banners` "a stated contract of `ship/run-program`" because a second reader depended on it (its `proposal.md:15`).

The alternative, the module reading `plan.json` and `state.json` itself, is closed twice over. The briefs' rejected-mechanisms list refuses the trajectory through `$.fs.read` as the meter's source because "the step stream crossing `tool.call` is cheaper and already parsed" (`briefs/claude-code-teams-and-orchestration-briefs.md:1351-1364`); the preflight sibling admits `$.fs.read` for one schema-stamped file and pins that the module spells none of `ship/runs`, `run.json`, `state.json` (`openspec/changes/show-preflight-and-interrupted-runs-at-session-start/design.md:148`); and the plan file lives under the state home `run start` resolves (`lib/run.mjs:120`, `:962-964`, `ship/state-home`), which a module with no Node cannot resolve. The step stream is the contract. What the board needs is added to it, sized, and pinned.

## What Changes

- **The relay contract widens by six named fields, and nothing else.** `RELAY_STEP_FIELDS` gains `waveIndex`, `batchIndex` and `batchCount` (three integers the step already carries, about 50 bytes on a batch or verify step); `skipped` (the boolean a verify step already carries, about 16 bytes); `recorded`, a new field on the step `run record-batch` returns, holding the ids the batch recorded ok, failed and not attempted, the same lists the trajectory's `record-batch` mutation already names (`lib/run.mjs:3040-3050`), about 10 bytes per id; and `plan`, a new field carried once on the step that first dispatches an adopted plan (`adoptPlan`, `:2581`) and again on the step after a replan rewrote the waves (`runReplan`, `:3482`), holding a summary of the run state's waves: per wave its index, group, kind, `red` and batches, per task only its id, tier, model and description cut as the lane title rule reads it, plus the `dependsOn` edges derived as `deferred[].after` is. About 110 bytes per task, 60 per wave and 40 per edge: under 3 KB for a twenty-task change, on one or two steps of a run. No `lanes` block: the plan's batches already list each lane's ids, and `laneLabel` derives the spawn's label from them, so the meter joins a spawn to its planned lane by label with no second list. Every other step grows by the three integers and nothing more. The step schema stays `interlock.run-step/1`: every field is additive and optional, a reader that ignores them reads today's step, and the schema string is pinned where it is today (`test/spine/run.test.mjs:3041`, `test/fixtures/mod/steps.mjs`). `bin/interlock-run` already receives the whole step and reads none of the new fields.
- **The summary is written once, Node-free, and draws as its plan.** A new `lib/plan-summary.mjs`, pure, importing only the sibling's `lib/lane.mjs`, turns a run state's waves (or a plan) into the `plan` field; `lib/run.mjs` imports it to build the field. The sibling's capability gains the requirement that makes this safe: the board reads of a task only what the summary keeps, pinned by a test that draws the fixture plan and its summary through the renderer and compares the lines for equality.
- **The meter pane draws the wave board as a section, not as a second pane.** The `waves` section of `/interlock-meter` becomes the board: one `Text` per line the renderer returns, `wrap: 'truncate-end'`, no colour, `Box`/`Text` only on both surfaces, cut at the pane's own `bodyColumns`. The board replaces the flat rows when a `plan` has crossed; the flat rows stay as the fallback beneath a spoken line when none has. The lane rows keep today's `wave-row-<label>` keys and today's live columns (served model, state), so every existing kit case holds. One pane, one command, no new open: a second pane behind `/interlock-board` would add a `command.run` matcher, a second unasked open the engine would seat as a tab the person has to find (the declaration's "one shown, the rest tabs"), and a second place to draw every lane. The status line and the spinner are unchanged.
- **The overlay comes from the steps alone.** The meter fills the renderer's overlay from what it holds: the cursor from the step's `action`, `waveIndex` and `batchIndex`; completed and failed ids accumulated from each `recorded` block; a skipped verification from a verify step carrying `skipped: true` and its `reason`, placed by `waveIndex` so two waves sharing a group never collide on the live board; the halt from the halt step. Per-lane live text (served models, the turn's reason) rides as notes keyed by lane label. The agent-to-lane join stays as it is, dormant until the host raises `agent.spawn` for Workflow agents (it did not on 2.1.289): rows read what the relayed plan says and agent figures stay `lane unknown`. Nothing is read from disk; `$.fs.read` is not called.
- **Degradation is spoken.** A run whose CLI relays no `plan` (an older `interlock` under a newer plugin) draws the words `plan structure not relayed by this CLI` where the board would be and the flat rows beneath, exactly as today. A pane narrower than the renderer's published minimum prints the renderer's one spoken line and the flat rows. A spawn whose label no planned lane carries is drawn as a flat row under the board, never dropped. The board names no cause and computes no verdict: every word is the step's, the renderer's or the CLI's.
- **Which pins move.** `ALLOWED_CALLS` is unchanged and `HOOKED_EVENTS` is unchanged: no new call, no new event, no new matcher. The import walker in `test/spine/mod-pins.test.mjs` covers `lib/draw-plan.mjs` and `lib/lane.mjs` by construction, so a renderer that reached Node would fail the pin before any session loaded the module; if the sibling's renderer were not Node-free this change could not import it and would stop at the relay. One new pin asserts the module imports the board from `../lib/draw-plan.mjs` and spells no `plan.json`. In `test/spine/run.test.mjs` the relay pin's "gone" list loses nothing and its kept list gains the six fields; `lanes` stays gone. `claude plugin validate --strict` records no new `hooks:` entry and no new `calls:` method; the design records the expected lines against the three preceding mod changes' lines.
- **Docs and the changelog.** `docs/07-cli-and-configuration.md` describes the board in the pane's list and corrects the sentence that the relay drops the batch fields; `docs/10-agentic-workflow-ship-and-spec.md` names the six relayed fields where it describes the step a driver obeys; `docs/13-the-guards.md` updates the `Bash` hook bullet and the pane bullet; `CHANGELOG.md` `[Unreleased]` carries the relay contract and the board; `briefs/claude-code-teams-and-orchestration-briefs.md` gains a dated note that the whitelist named as "a separate decision" in the meter's design was taken here, for the board, by six fields.
- **Probes come first.** (1) The widened fields survive the Workflow relay end to end: a stand-in `workflows/ship.js` under `scripts/host-probe.mjs --mode tty` (the memory recipe) runs the real `interlock run start --host workflow` and `run classified` through a mechanical ping and writes the relayed `cliStdout` to a scratch file; the probe records whether `plan`, `waveIndex`, `batchIndex`, `batchCount` arrived intact and the step's byte count. (2) The pane draws the board at 144 columns unasked and at the dock width when `/interlock-meter` is typed, on the terminal and, where available, the Desktop; and the kit's `Pane` props (`bodyColumns`, `placement`, `scroll.bodyRows`) on both surfaces drive the width-sensitive cut. Neither probe changes the spec; the first can change the field shape if a step of that size does not relay.
- **Serialization.** This change lands after `draw-wave-plan-and-handoff-graph-from-the-cli` (the renderer and `lib/lane.mjs`) and after the three mod changes that touch the same files (`speak-lane-turn-ends-and-session-cost`, `speak-permission-prompts-and-guard-denials`, `show-preflight-and-interrupted-runs-at-session-start`: `hooks/mod.mjs`, `test/mod/meter.test.ts`, `test/spine/mod-pins.test.mjs`, `docs/13-the-guards.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`); `observe-the-spec-run-live` lands after it.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None. The board is the sibling's `ship/diagrams` capability, drawn in a second place.

### Modified Capabilities

- `ship/run-program`: the requirement "The CLI SHALL emit every step of the lean run as a versioned record that names its own continuation" gains the relay contract: the six fields the Workflow host's relay carries for a second reader, their shapes, that the summary rides once per adoption and replan, that the schema identifier is unchanged, and that a driver reads none of them.
- `hooks/ship-meter`: the requirement "The meter SHALL read the run from the CLI's own step records, never from prose" gains the new fields the module takes off a step; the requirement "The pane SHALL draw on both surfaces and name the record it is not" gains the board section, its fallback and its width rule. The observe-only requirement's recorded hooks and calls do not change.
- `ship/diagrams` (the sibling's new capability, not re-declared here): two added requirements the pane needs and the renderer's proposal does not state — the board draws a plan summary exactly as the plan it summarises, and the board keys its rows and carries host-observed lane text for a second drawer.

## Impact

- **Changed:** `lib/run.mjs` (`RELAY_STEP_FIELDS`; `recorded` on the record-batch step; `plan` on the adoption and replan steps); `hooks/mod.mjs` (the plan and the recorded ids held on the run, the overlay, the board section with its fallback, the import); `test/spine/run.test.mjs` (the relay pin, the summary on the adoption and replan steps, `recorded` on the record-batch step, the schema string); `test/fixtures/mod/steps.mjs` (a relayed batch step with the fields, a record-batch step with `recorded`, a verify step with `skipped`); `test/mod/meter.test.ts` (the board on both surfaces, the fallback, the width cut, the overlay); `test/spine/mod-pins.test.mjs` (the import pin); `docs/07-cli-and-configuration.md`; `docs/10-agentic-workflow-ship-and-spec.md`; `docs/13-the-guards.md`; `CHANGELOG.md`; `briefs/claude-code-teams-and-orchestration-briefs.md`.
- **New:** `lib/plan-summary.mjs` (pure, Node-free; closure `lib/lane.mjs`) and `test/spine/plan-summary.test.mjs` (the summary's fields, its size against the plan, and the line-for-line equality through the sibling's renderer).
- **Untouched:** `workflows/ship.js` and `bin/interlock-run` (interpreters; neither reads a new field); every settings hook; `lib/limits.mjs` (no new key: the pane's width is the engine's `bodyColumns`, the renderer's minimum is the sibling's published key); `types/index.d.ts` (no new `$.state` key: the plan and the recorded ids are run state, reset with the run); `lib/draw-plan.mjs` and `lib/lane.mjs` beyond what the two added `ship/diagrams` requirements ask of the renderer; the trajectory, the manifest, the wave state and every corpus. No dependency.
- **Hosts:** on the Workflow host every relayed step grows by three integers, a batch's recording step by its ids, and one or two steps per run by the summary; the driver forwards them unread. On the runner hosts nothing changes: they already received the whole step. Below the mods floor nothing draws, as today.
