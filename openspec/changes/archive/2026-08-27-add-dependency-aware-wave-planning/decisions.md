| D1 | When a `dependsOn` edge and the numbered-section order disagree, which wins — do edges override sections, or are both honored? | agent_resolved | Both. Effective order is the union; sections stay a hard barrier and edges may only add ordering, never remove a section barrier. Overriding an author's section barrier is rejected as riskier and back-incompatible. | design.md §Decisions D1; explore brief §Pending Clarifications P1 |
| D2 | Does a `dependsOn` edge between path-disjoint tasks fold them into one serial lane, or order them as separate agents across waves/batches? | agent_resolved | Order-only; never fold. Folding path-disjoint tasks would violate the `lanes` invariant (lane = canonical-path collision component only) and inflate fat-lane replay cost. | design.md §Decisions D2; openspec/specs/lanes/spec.md "A lane SHALL contain only tasks joined by a canonical-path collision"; explore brief §Pending Clarifications P2 |
| D3 | How are edges realized in the planner — a new scheduling path, a new `packLanes` constraint, or reuse of the existing wave pipeline? | agent_resolved | Topological wave-depth over section ∪ edges feeding the existing wave→batch→lane→singleton-fold pipeline; the singleton-fold reclaims the checkpoint so a lone dependent buys no extra verify. | design.md §Decisions D3; openspec/specs/waves/spec.md "Singleton implementation waves fold onto the previous wave" |
| D4 | How are malformed edges (dangling reference, cycle, self-edge) handled — repaired silently or rejected? | agent_resolved | Rejected fail-closed in the planner's existing `validate`, naming the cause, mirroring the existing out-of-repo `paths` rejection. No silent drop or cycle-break. | design.md §Decisions D4; lib/waves.mjs:151-185 |
| D5 | Does this change infer dependency edges automatically from the codebase import/symbol graph? | agent_resolved | No — out of scope. | D6 | Does the planner auto-collapse over-incremented sections into parallel waves for throughput? | agent_resolved | No — out of scope. Sections remain a barrier; the parallelization win comes from the classifier needing fewer sections once a precise edge exists. | design.md §Decisions D6 |
