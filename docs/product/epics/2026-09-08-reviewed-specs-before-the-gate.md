- Who: software engineers improving their agentic coding workflow.
- Friction: spec-driven development is less effective than it could be, and
  many engineers prompt or vibe-code straight into a change with no reviewed
  spec in front of the human gate.
- Outcome: explore, propose, and artifact review produce a spec worth
  approving, so the human-in-the-loop gate has something real to gate on.
